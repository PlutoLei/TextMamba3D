The lesion area is in the brain primarily located in the right frontal lobe, with involvement of parts of the parietal lobe, with a mixed high and low signal with patchy high signal areas. Edema is significant, surrounding the lesions, especially in the right frontal lobe and is extensive. Necrosis is some low signal zones in parts of the lesion area, which are unevenly distributed with some concentration at the junction between the frontal and parietal lobes exhibiting mixed signals. Ventricular compression is the right-side lesion compressing the right ventricle.
