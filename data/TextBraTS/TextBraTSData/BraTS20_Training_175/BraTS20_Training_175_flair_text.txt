The lesion area is in the bilateral temporal and frontal lobes of the brain with a mix of heterogeneous high and low signals and spotty high-signal regions. Edema is significant high signals around the lesion areas, especially in the bilateral temporal and frontal lobes, consistent with edema signals. Necrosis is irregular low-signal regions in the images, mainly concentrated in the central location of the lesions. Ventricular compression is significant signs of compression, especially the right ventricle, which is notably deformed and narrowed due to pressure from the lesion and edema areas.
