The lesion area is in the bilateral frontal lobes and also involves the parietal and temporal lobes with heterogeneous signal characteristics, including mixed high and low signals and speckled high signal areas. Edema is significant, with signals mainly distributed around the lesion areas, particularly at the sites of lesions in the bilateral frontal lobes, spreading to surrounding tissues, indicating extensive swelling of tissues surrounding the lesions. Necrosis is observed with multiple low signal areas within the lesion regions, irregularly distributed and scattered throughout the lesion sites, with some areas concentrated at the core. Ventricular compression is notably observed with the right lateral ventricle appearing narrowed, indicating substantial pressure from the lesions on the ventricles.
