The lesion area is in the left cerebral hemisphere, particularly within the frontal and temporal lobes, with a mix of heterogeneous high-signal and low-signal regions, and spotted high-signal areas. Edema is observed as a pronounced high-signal in the frontal and temporal regions of the left cerebral hemisphere, surrounding the main lesions. Necrosis is suggested by low-signal areas within the left frontal and temporal lobes, consisting of low-signal patches of varying sizes and intermingle with the high-signal edema regions. Ventricular compression is notable, evidenced by the deformation and displacement of ventricular structures.
