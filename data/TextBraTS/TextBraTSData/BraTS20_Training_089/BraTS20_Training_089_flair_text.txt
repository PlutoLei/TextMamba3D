The lesion area is in the bilateral temporal and frontal lobes with heterogeneous mixed signals of varying intensity, accompanied by multiple patchy high signal areas. Edema is mainly observed around the left temporal lobe as an ill-defined high signal, with considerable extent affecting adjacent tissues near the right temporal lobe. Necrosis appears as a large low signal area in the left temporal and frontal lobes and scattered low signals in the right temporal and frontal lobes, indicating a severe degree of tissue necrosis. Ventricular compression is significant, particularly marked by deformation of the left lateral ventricle, which appears mildly compressed.
