The lesion area is in the right parietal lobe with a mixture of heterogeneous high and low signals with speckled high signal areas. Edema is significant, observed in the right parietal and frontal lobes, primarily located around the periphery of the lesion and covering a relatively large area. Necrosis is suggested by some regions of low signal intensity, mainly located in the central region of the lesions.
