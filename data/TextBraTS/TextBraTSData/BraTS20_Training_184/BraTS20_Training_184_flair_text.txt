The lesion area is in the right side of the brain, particularly in the temporal and frontal lobes, with a mixture of heterogeneous high and low signals and some patchy high signal regions. Edema is prominent near the right temporal and frontal lobes, characterized by high signal, extending broadly to the frontal-parietal region. Necrosis is indicated by some low signal areas mainly concentrated near the temporal lobe, presenting as heterogeneous signal regions with localized concentration. Ventricular compression is observed with the right ventricle noticeably compressed, resulting in deformation of the ventricular structure.
