The lesion area is in the right side of the brain, particularly in the frontal lobe, with a mixture of irregular high and low signals and significant enhancement at the edges of the lesion signals. Edema is clear, appearing in the right frontal lobe region and extends to the adjacent parietal and temporal lobes as a large region of uneven high signals. Necrosis is low signal areas within the high signal regions, primarily concentrated in the central part of the lesions with very low signal intensity, characterized by irregular zones of no signal. Ventricular compression is observed as the right lateral ventricle is slightly compressed, with a slight change in shape.
