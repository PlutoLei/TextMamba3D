The lesion area is in the left cerebral temporal and frontal lobes with a mixture of heterogeneous signals with speckled high-signal regions. Edema is observed in the left cerebral frontal lobe and parts of the temporal lobe with expansive high signals. Necrosis is indicated with lower signal intensity, considerable heterogeneity, and irregular shape within the temporal and frontal lobes. Ventricular compression is described due to the lesion area increasing pressure on the ventricles, particularly the lateral ventricles, resulting in partial compression.
