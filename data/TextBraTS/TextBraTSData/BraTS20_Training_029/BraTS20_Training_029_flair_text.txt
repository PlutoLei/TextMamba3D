The lesion area is in the right frontal and parietal lobes with mixed signals of varied intensities and patchy high-signal areas. Edema is observed in the right parietal lobe with blurred boundaries and is present in the right frontal lobe, although the extent is slightly smaller. Necrosis is present in the margins of the high-signal area in the right parietal lobe and several scattered areas in the right frontal lobe, characterized by uneven signal intensity and disorganized features. Ventricular compression is exerted on the ventricles, with observed changes in the shape of the left ventricle.
