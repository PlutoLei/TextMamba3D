The lesion area is in the left frontal lobe and the left temporal lobe with a mix of hyperintense and hypointense signals, with some spotty hyperintense areas. Edema is mainly observed around the lesion regions, particularly in the left temporal and frontal lobes, where these areas exhibit prominent hyperintense edema signals and extend over a broad area involving parts of the frontal and temporal lobes of the entire left hemisphere. Necrosis is observed in the deep left temporal lobe with a mixed signal appearance, including considerable low-signal necrotic area and unevenly distributed necrotic spots. Ventricular compression is seen in the left lateral ventricle, although the degree of compression is not significantly severe.
