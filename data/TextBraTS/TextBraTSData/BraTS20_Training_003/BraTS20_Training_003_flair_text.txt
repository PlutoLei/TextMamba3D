The lesion area is in the left frontal lobe, parietal lobe, and to a lesser extent, the temporal and occipital lobes, with a mixture of uneven signal intensities and patchy high signal areas. Edema is a prominent high signal edema area observed in the left frontal and parietal lobes, extending from the anterior part of the frontal lobe to the middle part of the parietal lobe, covering a large area. Necrosis is multiple regions with extremely low signal intensities found in the left frontal and parietal lobes, with irregular shapes and a scattered distribution primarily concentrated at the edges of the lesions. Ventricular compression is the left lateral ventricle being notably compressed and deformed, suggesting a significant compressive effect of the lesion on the brain tissue and ventricles.
