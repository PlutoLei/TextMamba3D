The lesion area is in multiple areas of the brain, presenting as mixed signals of varying intensities and speckled high-signal regions. Edema is significant, primarily seen in the left frontal and temporal lobes, with the edema extending over parts of the parietal and occipital lobes. Necrosis is possible in some regions, exhibiting highly heterogeneous signals, scattered and concentrated near the parietal and occipital lobes on the left side of the brain, characterized by high signal intensity and irregular boundaries. Ventricular compression is observed in parts of the ventricular system, particularly the anterior horns and the atrium of the lateral ventricles.
