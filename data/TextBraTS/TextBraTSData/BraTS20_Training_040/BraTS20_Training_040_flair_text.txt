The lesion area is in the bilateral frontal and temporal lobes with a mixed signal of uneven high and low intensity with patchy high signal areas. Edema is pronounced, chiefly observed in the bilateral lateral frontal lobes, with significant swelling of surrounding tissues, extensive and extending into the temporal lobes. Necrosis is located at the center of the bilateral lateral frontal lobes, characterized by high signal areas interspersed with low signals, primarily in the aforementioned areas. Ventricular compression is notably present as the right ventricle is compressed and deformed, whereas the left ventricle maintains a normal shape.
