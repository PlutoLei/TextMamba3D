The lesion area is in the left temporal and occipital lobes with heterogeneous mixed signals, with punctate high-signal areas. Edema is significant, with high signals around the lesions, mainly observed in the left temporal and occipital regions, extending to the periphery of the lesion areas, affecting the surrounding normal brain tissue and further intensifying the swelling. Necrosis is observed, showing mixed signals and are primarily located in the center and surrounding areas of the left temporal lobe lesion, presenting as low-signal regions interspersed with small amounts of high signal. Ventricular compression is a certain degree of compression on the left lateral ventricle, leading to deformation and reduction in volume of the left lateral ventricle.
