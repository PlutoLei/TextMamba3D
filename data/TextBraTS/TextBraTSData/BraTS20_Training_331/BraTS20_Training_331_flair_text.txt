The lesion area is in the temporal and occipital lobes of the left cerebral hemisphere with a mixture of heterogeneous high and low signals, with some areas showing patchy high signals. Edema is significant in the left temporal and occipital lobes, with blurred and diffuse boundaries; it covers a large area, including the temporal lobe and part of the occipital lobe. Necrosis is primarily concentrated in the left temporal lobe, manifested as low signal and mixed areas, scattered around the lesion area. Ventricular compression is observed as the left ventricle is compressed and deformed, but remains relatively normal.
