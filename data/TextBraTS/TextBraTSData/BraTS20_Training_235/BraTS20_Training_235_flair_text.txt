The lesion area is in the right frontal lobe region with a mixed signal of heterogeneous intensity and sporadic high-signal areas. Edema is significant around the lesion in the right frontal lobe, extensively spreading to adjacent areas within the right hemisphere and encroaching into parts of the temporal lobe. Necrosis is possible, with low-signal areas within the lesion, highly heterogeneous and primarily concentrated in the central part of the right frontal lobe, accompanied by scattered high-signal areas. Ventricular compression is observed with the right lateral ventricle compressed, having an irregular shape and reduced volume.
