The lesion area is in the frontal and parietal lobes on the left side of the brain with mixed heterogeneous signals with patchy high-signal areas. Edema is prominent and concentrated in the left frontal and parietal lobe areas, with considerable spreading to adjacent brain regions. Necrosis is characterized by mixed low signals and is relatively concentrated in the central region of the left frontal and parietal lobes. Ventricular compression is significant, with the left lateral ventricle being compressed and deformed.
