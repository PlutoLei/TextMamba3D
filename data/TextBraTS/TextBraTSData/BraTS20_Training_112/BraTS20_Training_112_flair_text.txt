The lesion area is in the right cerebral hemisphere, involving the right frontal lobe and the left parietal lobe with a mixture of heterogeneous high and low signals. Edema is present in the right cerebral hemisphere, particularly in the areas of the right frontal lobe and left parietal lobe, with extensive low signal changes and widespread coverage nearly over the entire periphery of the lesion areas. Necrosis is present with regions of extremely low signal intensity mainly concentrated within the right parietal lobe and right frontal lobe, displaying a mixed signal intensity. Ventricular compression is observed, with the right lateral ventricle noticeably compressed, showing deformation due to the pressure.
