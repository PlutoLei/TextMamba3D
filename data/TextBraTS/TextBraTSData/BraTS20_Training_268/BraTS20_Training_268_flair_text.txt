The lesion area is in the left cerebral region, including the frontal lobe, parietal lobe, and part of the temporal lobe, with a mixture of uneven high and low signals, with spotted areas of high signal. Edema is significant in the left frontal and parietal regions, with surrounding tissue markedly swollen and a considerable extent of swelling of tissues surrounding the lesion. Necrosis is within the lesion areas of the frontal and parietal lobes, represented by low signal areas with mixed signal intensity and scattered low signal regions. Ventricular compression is evidenced by compression and deformation of the left ventricle due to the impact of the lesion on brain tissue and ventricles.
