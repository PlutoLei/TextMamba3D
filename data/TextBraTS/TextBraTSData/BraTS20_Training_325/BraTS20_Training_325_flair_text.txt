The lesion area is in the bilateral parietal and frontal lobes with mixed signal intensities of high and low and spotted high signal areas. Edema is mainly observed around the bilateral parietal lobes, indicating a high degree of swelling in the surrounding tissue of the lesion, and extends to parts of the temporal lobe. Necrosis is present within the regions of mixed high and low signals, concentrated at the center of the lesions, with some areas possibly showing reduced signals due to a lack of blood supply. Ventricular compression is noteworthy, with particular attention to the increased pressure in the lateral ventricles.
