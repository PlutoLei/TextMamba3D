The lesion area is in the left parietal and occipital lobes with mixed signals of varying intensity, characterized by irregular spots and patchy high-signal areas. Edema is diffuse high-signal areas in the left parietal and occipital lobes, with significant expansion into surrounding areas. Necrosis is some marked regions with distinct low-signal features, mainly concentrated in the left parietal lobe and parts of the occipital-like region, exhibiting a mix of low and high signals. Ventricular compression is some compression of the ventricular structures, with signs of ventricular deformation and compression in the significantly affected areas of the left parietal and occipital lobes.
