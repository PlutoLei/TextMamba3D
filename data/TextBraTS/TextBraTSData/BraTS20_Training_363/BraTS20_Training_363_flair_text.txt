The lesion area is in the left frontal and temporal lobes with a mixed pattern of heterogeneous high and low signals with spotted high-signal areas. Edema is extensive, as the left frontal and temporal lobes display marked high-signal areas that are not confined to the primary lesions but also extend to surrounding normal tissue. Necrosis is observed with low-signal areas within some high-signal regions, mainly distributed in the deep part of the left temporal lobe and parts of the subcortical regions of the frontal lobe, exhibiting mixed signals. Ventricular compression is noted with the ventricular system on the left side widened, especially evident in the left lateral ventricle.
