The lesion area is in the right hemisphere of the brain with a mix of heterogeneous high and low signals and patchy high-signal regions. Edema is significant high-signal, observed in parts of the right hemisphere surrounding the lesions, including sections of the frontal and temporal lobes. Necrosis is mixed signals with uneven intensity, displaying patchy or diffuse combinations of high and low signals, dispersed within the lesions. Ventricular compression is observable compression of the ventricles, particularly the right ventricle area, by the lesion area.
