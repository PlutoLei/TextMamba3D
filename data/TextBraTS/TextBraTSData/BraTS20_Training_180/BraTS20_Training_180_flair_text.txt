The lesion area is in almost all regions of the brain, including the right frontal, temporal, parietal, and occipital lobes with a mixture of uneven high and low signals. Edema is pronounced, mainly concentrated in the right frontal and occipital lobes, presenting as swelling of the surrounding brain tissue with a relatively large range. Necrosis is observed in the right parietal and temporal lobes, appearing as low signal regions compared to normal brain tissue, exhibiting noticeable signal heterogeneity. Ventricular compression is observed particularly in the parietal and occipital surrounding ventricular areas, where the compression is most severe.
