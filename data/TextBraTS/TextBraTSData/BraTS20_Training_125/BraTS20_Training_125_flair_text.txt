The lesion area is in the frontal and parietal lobes of the right cerebral hemisphere with a mix of heterogeneous high and low signals with speckled high-intensity signals. Edema is primarily located around the lesions, especially in the frontal and parietal lobes of the right cerebral hemisphere, presenting as notably high intensity. Necrosis is primarily located in the deeper structures of the right frontal and parietal lobes with lower signal intensity compared to the higher signal intensity areas of edema. Ventricular compression is some compression effect on the ventricles by the right hemisphere lesions, but no significant changes in the shape of the ventricles have been observed.
