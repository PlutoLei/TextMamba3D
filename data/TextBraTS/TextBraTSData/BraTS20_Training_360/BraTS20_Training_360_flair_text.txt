The lesion area is in the frontal, parietal, and temporal lobes of the right cerebral hemisphere with a mixture of high and low heterogeneous signals with patchy high signal regions. Edema is significant from the right frontal lobe to the parietal lobe, with partial involvement of the temporal lobe, mainly presenting high signals and covering a broad area. Necrosis is located deep within the right frontal and parietal lobes, displaying heterogeneous low signals and scattered in distribution. Ventricular compression is evident deformation of the right lateral ventricle due to compression.
