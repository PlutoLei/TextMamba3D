The lesion area is in the left frontal and parietal lobes with multiple mixed high and low signal areas and speckled high signal regions, distributed unevenly with indistinct boundaries. Edema is significant with notable swelling in the tissue around the lesions, surrounding the left frontal and parietal lobes and affecting the normal brain tissue around these areas. Necrosis is observed in the central part of the lesion area as a large low signal region, mainly concentrated within the right frontal lobe. Ventricular compression is observed as the left lateral ventricle is noticeably compressed and deformed, consistent with the location of the lesions, suggesting a certain degree of compression effect on the ventricles caused by the lesions.
