The lesion area is in the left frontal lobe, left parietal lobe, and right frontal lobe with a mix of heterogeneous high and low signal areas, as well as speckled high signal regions. Edema is observed around the lesions, extending over a large area from the anterior portion of the frontal lobe to the posterior part of the parietal lobe, covering a vast region of brain tissue, predominantly showing high signals. Necrosis is present, with mixed signal characteristics, located primarily in the left frontal lobe and left parietal lobe. Ventricular compression is significant in the left lateral ventricle, resulting in deformation and displacement, while the right lateral ventricle exhibits relatively mild compression but still shows some degree of deformation.
