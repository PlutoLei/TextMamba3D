The lesion area is in the left and right frontal lobes with irregular mixed high and low signals and spotted high signal areas, and scattered high signal areas are present in both the left and right temporal lobes, and the parietal lobes show mixed signals with lacunar areas, and the primary area is the right occipital lobe with spotted high signal regions. Edema is present in the left frontal lobe with significant signals, covering a large portion, small amount seen in parietal, and less pronounced but observable in the temporal and occipital lobes. Necrosis is observed in the left frontal lobe with mixed low and high signals suggesting potential necrotic regions, and some low signal areas in the parietal and few spotted low signal regions in the temporal and occipital lobes. Ventricular compression is described as undergoing varying degrees of compression, mainly due to lesions in the left temporal and frontal lobes, resulting in ventricular reduction.
