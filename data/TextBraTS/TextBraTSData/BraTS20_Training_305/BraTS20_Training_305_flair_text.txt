The lesion area is in the right cerebral hemisphere, particularly the parietal and occipital lobes, with a mix of heterogeneous high and low signals, along with speckled high-signal areas. Edema is notable high-signal areas surrounding the lesions mainly concentrated in the right parietal and occipital lobes, with significant swelling of the surrounding tissues, covering a large area. Necrosis is some low-signal areas with uneven signal intensity, with some areas exhibiting extremely low signals, both concentrated and somewhat dispersed. Ventricular compression is the right ventricle subjected to varying degrees of compression, resulting in slight deformation.
