The lesion area is in the frontal and temporal lobes of the left cerebral hemisphere with heterogeneous high-signal and speckled high-signal areas. Edema is significant, with large high-signal areas mainly observed in the left cerebral hemisphere surrounding the frontal and temporal lobes, extending over the entire left hemisphere. Necrosis is suggested by a large low-signal area in the left temporal lobe with mixed signal intensity characteristics, primarily clustered in the temporal lobe.
