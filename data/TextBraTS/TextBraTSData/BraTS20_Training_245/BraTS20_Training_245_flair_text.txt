The lesion area is in the left cerebral hemisphere, particularly in the frontal and parietal lobes, with mixed signals of uneven high and low intensity. Edema is significant, observed in the left frontal and parietal regions, extending to adjacent white matter areas and presents as high-intensity signals. Necrosis is suspected in the low signal regions within the lesion area, particularly in the mixed high and low signal area of the central parietal lobe, showing as low signal areas mixed into the background of patchy high signals. Ventricular compression is observed, with the left lateral ventricle showing some degree of constriction and deformation.
