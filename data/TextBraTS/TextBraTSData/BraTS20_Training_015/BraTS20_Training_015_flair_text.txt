The lesion area is in the middle region of the left temporal lobe and the middle anterior part of the left frontal lobe, with mixed signals of varying intensities along with patchy high-signal areas. Edema is predominantly found in the middle part of the left temporal lobe and the middle anterior part of the right frontal lobe, with adjacent tissues significantly swollen, affecting a large area of brain parenchyma. Necrosis is mainly situated in the middle part of the left temporal lobe, characterized by a mixture of low and iso-signals with irregular mixed signals at the edges. Ventricular compression is observed with deformation of the ventricles, particularly the left ventricle, under pressure from the lesions.
