The lesion area is in the right hemisphere of the brain, near the frontal and parietal lobes, with a heterogeneous mix of high and low signals, and patchy high signal areas. Edema is significant, mainly around the lesion sites, particularly in the posterior part of the right frontal lobe and the medial part of the right parietal lobe. Necrosis is observed as low signal images, with heterogeneous signals concentrated at the junction of the parietal and frontal lobes in the right hemisphere. Ventricular compression is not observed, as the ventricular morphology is normal, showing no clear signs of deformation or compression.
