The lesion area is in the frontal and parietal lobes of the left cerebral hemisphere with a mix of heterogeneous high and low signals. Edema is mainly localized in the surrounding tissue of the lesions, especially near the frontal lobe, manifesting as high signal regions. Necrosis is mainly located in the core of the lesions, exhibiting low or very low signals, and these necrotic regions are mostly concentrated in the central parts. Ventricular compression is noticeable, particularly the deformation of the lateral ventricles, indicating significant pressure effects on the ventricular system from the lesions.
