The lesion area is in the frontal lobe and temporal lobe with mixed signals of varying intensities and patchy high signal regions. Edema is observed with uniform high signal regions around the frontal lobe suggesting swelling of the surrounding tissue, with a significant range of edema; also present in small parts of the parietal and occipital lobes with relatively mild edema. Necrosis is observed in some small scattered areas of the parietal and occipital lobes characterized by lower signal intensity, with the degree quite mixed. Ventricular compression is relatively localized, primarily affecting the area in the heavily affected frontal lobe, resulting in a degree of compressive deformation.
