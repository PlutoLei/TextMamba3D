The lesion area is in the right frontal and occipital lobes with heterogeneous mixed signals and patchy high signals. Edema is significant, mainly observed in the right frontal lobe and part of the occipital lobe with diffuse high signal intensity, extending into surrounding normal tissue. Necrosis is seen in the central part of the right frontal lobe, characterized by low signal mixed with high signal plaques. Ventricular compression is evident in the right lateral ventricle, particularly in the anterior horn region.
