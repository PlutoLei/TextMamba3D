## Consensus Findings

*What multiple papers agree on, with supporting citations.*

1. **Text guidance CAN improve 3D brain tumor segmentation, but only with the right fusion strategy.** TextBraTS demonstrated +1.5% Dice (83.8% to 85.3%, p=0.0077) on the same BraTS2020 dataset with bidirectional cross-attention at bottleneck only. Lemay et al. showed +5.1% with FiLM conditioning using simple categorical metadata. LViT showed text compensation in semi-supervised settings. The consistent finding is that text helps when fusion is correctly designed -- not that text always helps.
   -- Supported by: TextBraTS (Li et al., 2025), Lemay et al. (2021), LViT (Li et al., 2023)

2. **Cross-attention outperforms FiLM for high-dimensional text conditioning.** Neural Field Conditioning Strategies (2023) directly compared FiLM vs cross-attention vs concatenation and found Cross-Attention > FiLM > Concatenation. TextBraTS confirmed this in the medical imaging domain: bidirectional cross-attention (85.3%) beat dot-sum fusion (81.6%) by 3.7 percentage points. Critically, FiLM was designed for and validated with low-dimensional conditioning signals (categorical metadata, class labels), not 768-dimensional BERT embeddings.
   -- Supported by: Neural Field Conditioning (2023), TextBraTS (2025), Lemay et al. (2021)

3. **Injection site matters: bottleneck-only is safer than all-encoder-stages.** TextBraTS injected text at H/32 resolution (bottleneck only) and achieved positive results. TextMamba3D applies Multi-Scale FiLM at ALL 4 encoder stages (dims 48, 96, 192, 384), which forces every encoder layer to accommodate text modulation before the visual features have been adequately extracted. No surveyed paper applied text conditioning at all encoder stages successfully.
   -- Supported by: TextBraTS (2025), LViT (2023, text fused through separate ViT branch, not early encoder)

4. **Region-level alignment is superior to instance-level for medical image-text learning.** GLoRIA demonstrated that contrasting image sub-regions with paired report words captures clinically meaningful spatial correspondences. TextMamba3D's contrastive loss (currently disabled) was designed at instance level, which is insufficient for the spatially heterogeneous nature of brain tumors.
   -- Supported by: GLoRIA (2021), TextBraTS (2025, location-specific text improves region-specific Dice)

5. **Mamba SSM architectures are competitive for 3D medical segmentation, establishing the backbone as viable.** VSSD-UNet, SegMamba, and the "What can Mamba do for 3D" study all confirm SSM-based segmentation works. The text injection mechanism is the problem, not the Mamba backbone.
   -- Supported by: Idea2Paper KG (27 papers on Mamba medical segmentation)

## Contradictions and Debates

| Claim | Supporting Papers | Contradicting Papers | Likely Reason |
|-------|-------------------|----------------------|---------------|
| FiLM is effective for medical segmentation conditioning | Lemay et al. (2021): +5.1% with categorical metadata | TextMamba3D (internal): -0.38% with BERT embeddings; Neural Field Conditioning (2023): cross-attention > FiLM | **Dimensionality mismatch.** Lemay used one-hot categorical vectors (dim ~5-10); TextMamba3D feeds 256-dim projected BERT embeddings through FiLM. FiLM's affine transform (gamma * x + beta) cannot selectively route high-dimensional, spatially-relevant information. It works as a global channel scaler, not a spatial attention mechanism. |
| Text should be injected at multiple scales | TextMamba3D design rationale: multi-scale FiLM at 4 stages for hierarchical conditioning | TextBraTS (2025): bottleneck-only injection achieves SOTA | **Early-stage corruption.** At shallow encoder layers, visual features are low-level (edges, textures). Modulating them with semantic text information introduces noise before the model has extracted meaningful spatial representations. Bottleneck injection allows text to interact with high-level, semantically rich features. |
| Frozen text encoders are sufficient | TextBraTS (2025): frozen BioBERT + MLP projection works well | TextMamba3D (internal): frozen PubMedBERT features may not align with Mamba feature space | **Projection capacity gap.** TextBraTS uses MLP projection into a cross-attention mechanism with learned query/key/value matrices, which has sufficient capacity to bridge the domain gap. TextMamba3D uses a thin projection (Linear + LN + GELU) into FiLM gamma/beta generators, which has far less adaptive capacity. The fusion mechanism, not the encoder, is the bottleneck. |

## Key Insights for Implementation

*5 actionable insights directly relevant to fixing TextMamba3D's text injection problem.*

1. **The most impactful single change is replacing Multi-Scale FiLM with bottleneck-only cross-attention.** The convergent evidence from TextBraTS (direct comparison on same dataset), Neural Field Conditioning (systematic comparison across fusion methods), and Lemay et al. (FiLM only works for low-dim conditioning) all point to the same conclusion: FiLM is the wrong fusion mechanism for high-dimensional BERT embeddings, and text should enter at the bottleneck, not at every encoder stage. Implementation: remove `MultiScaleFiLM` calls from stages 1-3 in `textmamba3d.py`, implement a `BidirectionalCrossAttention` module at bottleneck (dim=384, text_dim=256, num_heads=8), and feed projected PubMedBERT output as key/value.

2. **CausalMambaFusion's "text-first" design is likely harmful.** By prepending text tokens to the image sequence, the SSM's hidden state is initialized by text context before seeing any visual input. This means every image token's state carries a text-derived bias accumulated through the causal scan. In contrast, bidirectional cross-attention allows image features to selectively attend to relevant text tokens without corrupting the sequential visual processing. The fix: replace CausalMambaFusion with cross-attention, or at minimum, move text tokens to the END of the sequence (so visual state is established first, then text refines it).

3. **The default_text_embed paradox must be resolved before any fusion fix can be validated.** When no_text_ratio triggers no-text mode, the model uses a learned `default_text_embed` (nn.Parameter). After LayerNorm, this becomes a stable, low-entropy vector that produces safe FiLM parameters (gamma near 1, beta near 0). Real PubMedBERT outputs are higher-entropy and produce more variable FiLM parameters, which at early training stages may be destructive. Implementation check: log the L2 norm and variance of FiLM gamma/beta for text-conditioned vs default-conditioned batches to confirm this hypothesis.

4. **TextBraTS's template format should be adopted for fair comparison.** TextBraTS showed templated text (85.3%) outperforms raw text (84.6%) by 0.7%. Since TextMamba3D uses the same TextBraTS dataset, adopting the same template format eliminates text quality as a confound. Additionally, TextBraTS's finding that "location info improves WT Dice" and "feature descriptions improve edge HD95" suggests that different text components have region-specific value -- a cross-attention mechanism can learn to route these, but FiLM cannot.

5. **Run the Pure Vision ablation BEFORE implementing any fix.** The -0.38% delta (with-text vs without-text) is small enough to be within noise. Before investing engineering effort in cross-attention replacement, train a Pure Vision model (completely remove text encoder, FiLM, and MambaFusion) on the A100 with 128^3 patches. If Pure Vision achieves comparable or better Dice than the full model, this definitively confirms the text mechanism is harmful and justifies the architectural overhaul. If with-text catches up or surpasses after longer training on A100 (where patch_size=128^3 provides adequate visual context), the problem may have been the 64^3 patch size, not the fusion mechanism.
